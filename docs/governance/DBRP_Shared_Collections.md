# Disaster Backup & Recovery Protocols (DBRP) for Shared Collections

## 1. Overview
When private partners (Syndicates) share ownership of physical assets (trading cards, sealed wax), physical loss, damage, or sudden life events pose a critical risk. The DBRP ensures the collection, its value, and its legacy are preserved.

## 2. Digital Twin Backup (The ACool App)
- Every card entered into a Syndicate is scanned via the ACoolAPP.
- High-res images, grading certificates, and purchase receipts are uploaded to the Google Cloud Storage backend.
- The "Digital Twin" proves ownership and condition in the event of an insurance claim.

## 3. Physical Vaulting & Insurance
- High-value Syndicate assets (Pinnacle/Gem Tiers) must be stored in a certified vault (e.g., PWCC Vault, Alt) or a localized bank safety deposit box.
- The DBRP requires all Syndicate pools to allocate 1% of their Escrow funds toward specialized collectibles insurance.

## 4. Continuity & Beneficiary Protocol
Tied directly to **The CAUSE (Commission of Continuity)**:
- In the event a Syndicate member passes away, their fractional ownership is immediately transferred to their registered beneficiary or Living Trust.
- The surviving Syndicate members have the first right of refusal to buy out the beneficiary's share at the current Omnianalysis market rate.

# ACoolOMNI: The Omnianalysis Trading Card Framework

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy
The Omnianalysis framework is the definitive architectural blueprint for **ACoolCOLLECTOR**. It bridges cutting-edge data aggregation with the profound personal discipline, memory, and standard of care inspired by the "Ruth Review" phase. It is an investment in the future, built on the legacy of the past.

## 2. The Data Ingestion Engine (ACoolOSINT & ACoolAPI)
To create a true "Omnianalysis", the system must aggregate and normalize data across the entire hobby ecosystem.
- **Pricing & Market Aggregators:** SportsCardsPro API, 130point (sales history), CardLadder (indexes), MarketMovers (trends).
- **Gaming & Retail:** TCGplayer (TCG standard), Fanatics Live (breaks/auctions), Fanatics Shops, GameStop.
- **Grading & Authentication Houses:** PSA (values/pop reports), Beckett (BGS/BCCG), SGC, CGC, TAG (transparent grading).

## 3. The Omnianalysis Grading Matrix
A core feature of the analysis is standardizing grading scales to find market arbitrage and true value.
- **The Pinnacle Tier:** BGS Black Label, SGC Pristine 10 (Gold Label), CGC Perfect 10, TAG 1000.
- **The Gem Tier:** PSA 10 (Gem Mint), BGS 9.5 (Gem Mint), SGC 10 (Gem), CGC 10 (Pristine/Gem Mint), TAG 950-990.
- **The Mint Tier:** PSA 9, BGS 9, SGC 9, CGC 9, TAG 900.
- **Factors:** Centering, Corners, Edges, Surface. (Special emphasis on BGS subgrades and TAG's objective digital reports).

## 4. The Development & Review Pipeline
The deployment lifecycle is structured to ensure absolute excellence before reaching the public.

### Phase 1: The Alpha Protocol
- **Objective:** Initial system build using ACoolSCRIPT and ACoolAGENT.
- **Deliverable:** Working internal prototype connecting SportsCardsPro API, basic UI, and the unified grading matrix.

### Phase 2: The Ruth Review
- **Objective:** The standard-setting QA phase. Named in honor and memory, this phase is governed by discipline, absolute truth, and ensuring the foundation is flawless. Does the system serve the collector with integrity?
- **Deliverable:** A hardened, emotionally and technically validated release candidate.

### Phase 3: The Beth Beta
- **Objective:** The presentation-ready build.
- **Deliverable:** A polished beta environment featuring full Omnianalysis charts, historical data mapping, and live API feeds.

### Phase 4: The Board of Barbara
- **Objective:** Industry validation and executive feedback.
- **Participants:** Board of Advisors, Reviewers, Testers, Industry Experts, and Corporate Executives.
- **Deliverable:** Executive buy-in, partnership readiness (Fanatics, GameStop, grading houses), and final production greenlight.

## 5. Technology Stack Alignment
- **ACoolAGENT:** Orchestrates the automated fetching from 130point and TCGplayer.
- **ACoolOSINT:** Scrapes and analyzes Fanatics Live trends and retail availability.
- **ACoolAI:** Powers the Omnianalysis engine, predicting card values based on population reports and grading cycles.
- **ACoolSCRIPT:** Automates the daily data synchronization and database backups.

# ACoolCOMMUNITY_RASCI Matrix

To ensure the ACoolECOSYSTEM evolves efficiently and engages the entire trading card world (and beyond), we define roles using the RASCI framework:
- **R (Responsible):** Those who do the work to complete the task.
- **A (Accountable):** The ultimate owner of the outcome.
- **S (Support):** Those who provide resources or play a supporting role.
- **C (Consulted):** Those whose opinions are sought before decisions are made.
- **I (Informed):** Those who are kept up-to-date on progress.

## Roles Defined
1. **ACoolOMNI (Engine):** The AI-driven brain, evolving and contextualizing the ecosystem.
2. **ACoolNERD / Core Team:** The architects and primary builders.
3. **Collectors:** Individual enthusiasts and portfolio managers.
4. **Vendors (LCS / Online):** Local Card Shops and digital storefronts.
5. **Distributors / Breakers:** Bulk handlers and content creators.
6. **Subcommunities (CoOp COLLECTORS):** Localized or niche groups hosting events.

# Griggs Governance: The Supreme Charter

## 1. The Namesake & Philosophy
The supreme governance structure of the ACoolECOSYSTEM is officially named **Griggs Governance**, honoring the maiden name of ACoolNERD's mother. This ensures that every rule, protocol, and code block is infused with her legacy of discipline, care, and integrity.

## 2. Structural Hierarchy
Griggs Governance encapsulates all executive and operational boards within the ecosystem:
1. **The Legacy Core:** The foundational philosophy dictating that all commerce must serve a higher purpose (The CAUSE).
2. **The Board of Barbara:** The executive council (ACoolNERD, Advisors, Executives) that votes on and implements the strategy under the Griggs mandate.
3. **The Ruth Review:** The rigorous Quality Assurance and Integrity standard that all data must pass before approval.

## 3. The Core Mandate
Under Griggs Governance, ACoolCOLLECTOR operates strictly as a "For-Profit for Good." The governance oversees the distribution of assets into the 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(4) entities, ensuring the community is continually edified and protected.

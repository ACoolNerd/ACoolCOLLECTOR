# The Bridge by BETH: Omni-Customer & Market Analysis

## 1. Overview
The Bridge by BETH is the intelligence conduit of the ACoolECOSYSTEM. It bridges the gap between raw data (Alpha Protocol) and human sentiment. It ensures the Board of Barbara is making decisions based on both hard analytics and the actual pulse of the community.

## 2. Omni-Customer Presence
The Bridge aggregates sentiment from across the hobby:
- **Professional Insight:** Scrapes and analyzes data from CardLadder, SportsCardsPro, and Fanatics Live market reports.
- **Customer Insight:** Parses blogs, Reddit (r/basketballcards, r/PokemonTCG), Blowout Forums, and CoOp COLLECTOR internal feedback.

## 3. The Analytics Engine (BETH)
- **Trend Detection:** Identifies micro-trends before they become macro-shifts (e.g., "A specific UFC fighter is trending on blogs before their rookie card spikes on 130point").
- **Content Generation:** Feeds professional insights back to the CoOp through the `WEEKLY_MARKET_SNAPSHOT.md`, ensuring our collectors are the most informed in the market.

## 4. Subgroup Integration
BETH acts as the bridge connecting the Distributors (Macro) to the Vendors (Retail) to the Collectors (Micro), ensuring Omni Presence across the entire value chain.

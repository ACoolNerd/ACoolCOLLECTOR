# The Mutual Fulfillment Profile (MFP)

## 1. Ethical Engine: "Think Win-Win"
Inspired by the 7 Habits of Highly Effective People, the **Mutual Fulfillment Profile (MFP)** is the ethical engine driving all commerce within ACoolCOLLECTOR. It guarantees that every transaction, trade, or syndicate break results in mutual benefit.

## 2. FAITH of FULFILLMENT
In financial investments and collectibles, trust is the ultimate currency. The MFP algorithm scores transactions to ensure **FAITH of FULFILLMENT**:
- **F**airness in pricing (verified by the Omnianalysis API).
- **A**ccountability in shipping/vaulting.
- **I**ntegrity in grading (Ruth Review standard).
- **T**ransparency in fractional ownership.
- **H**onesty in market trends (Bridge by BETH).

## 3. Protocol Enforcement
If an ACoolOMNI scan detects a "Win-Lose" scenario (e.g., a Vendor pricing a raw card 40% above the SportsCardsPro market index without a verified grade), the MFP automatically flags the transaction for review by the Board of Barbara to protect the Collector.
